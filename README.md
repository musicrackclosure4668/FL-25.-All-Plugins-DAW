## 🎓 How to Use FL 25 for Beginners

### Creating Your First Beat

1. Open FL 25 All Plugins DAW
2. Click on the step sequencer at the bottom of the screen
3. Select a drum sound from the channel rack
4. Click on steps in the pattern to place drum hits
5. Press Play to hear your beat

### Adding a Melody

1. Right-click on an empty channel in the channel rack
2. Choose "Insert" and select a synthesizer
3. Click the piano roll icon next to the channel
4. Draw notes on the grid to create your melody
5. Press Play to hear both the beat and melody together

### Recording Audio

1. Connect a microphone or instrument to your computer
2. Create a new audio track in the playlist
3. Arm the track for recording
4. Press the record button
5. Play your instrument or sing

## 🎚️ Mixing Your Music

The mixing process balances all your sounds and makes them work together. Here is a simple workflow:

1. Open the mixer window (F9)
2. Route each channel to its own mixer track
3. Adjust volume levels so nothing clips
4. Add EQ to clean up frequencies
5. Add compression to control dynamics
6. Use reverb and delay for space
7. Pan instruments across the stereo field

## 🎚️ Mastering Your Final Track

When your mix sounds good, it is time to master:

1. Export your mix as a high-quality audio file
2. Open a new project and import that file
3. Place Maximus on the master channel
4. Use the presets as starting points
5. Adjust to match your desired loudness
6. Export the final mastered version

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Audio Crackling or Stuttering

- Increase the buffer size in audio settings
- Close other programs that use the audio device
- Update your audio drivers
- Use ASIO drivers if available

### Plugin Not Loading

- Make sure you have the correct VST3 folder selected
- Restart FL 25 after installing new plugins
- Check that your system meets requirements
- Try running FL 25 as administrator

### Project Won't Save

- Check disk space on your drive
- Make sure you have write permissions to the folder
- Save to a different location
- Export a backup copy
